About the Studio

SOLO DEVELOPMENT // SUNDERLAND, UK

A/D
Alastis Dev is a one-person indie studio building dark fantasy games with engineering precision. Founded by Marek Banas — self-taught developer based in Sunderland, UK. Every game ships with no ads and no in-app purchases — you either get it free or you buy it once, and that's the end of it. The mission: prove that limitations breed creativity, and that games can respect your attention instead of farming it.
