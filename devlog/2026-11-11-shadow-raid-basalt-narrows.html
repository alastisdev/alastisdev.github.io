2026.11.11 SHADOW_RAID GODOT ACT II

Flying the Basalt Narrows in Shadow Raid

The dotted line on the right wall gives me just enough time to pull back. Once the flame dies, I can slip through the gap.

Room to retreat

I wanted the tunnel to feel tight on a portrait screen. Most of the time I dodge sideways, but here a rock wall already occupies the space I’d normally use. The warning line lets me trade a little forward progress for a safe opening. The clip shows the whole manoeuvre in twelve seconds.

Beyond the tunnel

The first two missions take place between rock walls. Farther down, the ground gives way to lava and small volcanoes throw fireballs up towards the ship. Past the tunnel, the passage opens into a chamber where the pirate boss is waiting.

I’ve played through all seven Act II missions on PC. Next I’m taking my Tier 2 ship back to the first mission to tune the difficulty. I still have the Pixel 7a playtest ahead of me.
